Company Overview

Delivering Quality Products Across Multiple Industries

Santosh Manufacturing Units Pvt Ltd is a manufacturer and importer specializing in Home Appliances, Decorative Lighting, Interior Decor Products, and Plywood. With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we serve a growing network of distributors, dealers, and wholesalers across India.

Our diverse portfolio enables us to provide complete solutions for modern homes, commercial spaces, and hospitality projects. All our products are ISI, BIS, CE, RoHS, and ISO certified, ensuring dependable performance and compliance with the highest industry standards.

Our Story

Brand Journey

From trading roots to a full-scale manufacturing and import enterprise serving dealers and distributors across India.

Phase 1
The Beginning

Established Santosh Manufacturing Units Pvt Ltd with a focus on quality product sourcing and B2B distribution across India.

Phase 2
Plywood Division

Expanded into plywood, offering ply board, doors, and HDMR products to meet the demands of construction and furniture manufacturers.

Phase 3
POWERLAM Brand Launch

Launched the POWERLAM brand for Decorative Lighting and Interior Decor Solutions — available on Amazon, Flipkart, and major e-commerce platforms.

Phase 4
Home Appliances Manufacturing

Entered home appliances manufacturing with a product range including induction cooktops, infrared cooktops, air fryers, tea machines, and coffee machines.

Today
Pan India Distribution

Serving dealers, distributors, and wholesalers across India with ISI, BIS, CE, RoHS, and ISO certified products and a nationwide support network.

Quality Commitment

Certifications & Compliance

Every product is selected and tested to meet defined quality standards, ensuring dependable performance and customer satisfaction. Our products carry internationally recognized certifications.

ISI Certified

Products bearing the ISI mark comply with Indian Standards Institution requirements, assuring quality and safety for the Indian market.

BIS Certified

Bureau of Indian Standards certification confirms compliance with Indian product safety and quality regulations.

CE Certified

CE marking indicates conformity with European health, safety, and environmental protection standards for global market confidence.

RoHS Compliant

Restriction of Hazardous Substances compliance ensures our products are free from dangerous materials, protecting people and the environment.

ISO Certified

ISO certification demonstrates our commitment to consistent quality management systems across all operations and product categories.

Strict Quality Control

Multi-stage inspection processes at every level of production guarantee that our products meet rigorous standards before reaching customers.

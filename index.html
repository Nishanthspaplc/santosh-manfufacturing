About Us

Delivering Quality Products Across Multiple Industries

Santosh Manufacturing Units Pvt Ltd is a manufacturer and importer specializing in Home Appliances, Decorative Lighting, Interior Decor Products and Plywood.

With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we serve a growing network of distributors, dealers, and wholesalers across India. Our diverse portfolio enables us to provide complete solutions for modern homes, commercial spaces, and hospitality projects.

  • ISI, BIS, CE, RoHS & ISO certified products
  • Manufacturing & import expertise with strong sourcing capabilities
  • Consistent quality control across all product categories
  • Nationwide dealer & distributor support network

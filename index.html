About Us

Your Trusted Supplier for Premium Home Appliances

Santosh Manufacturing Units Pvt Ltd is a manufacturer and importer of certified home appliances — MK-series espresso coffee makers, air fryers (4L–15L), induction cooktops, and tea machines — serving dealers across India since 2001.

With a focus on quality and innovation, we supply a growing network of distributors, dealers, and wholesalers nationwide. Our home appliances are tested to meet defined standards, ensuring reliable performance and long-term customer satisfaction for every household.

  • ISI, BIS, CB, CE, RoHS, LFGB & BSCI certified appliances
  • Direct manufacturer & importer — competitive B2B pricing
  • MK-series espresso makers, air fryers (4L–15L), tea & induction cooktops
  • Nationwide dealer & distributor support network
